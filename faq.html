Why Career Compass?

Benefits of studying with Career compass

At Career Compass, we provide premier ACCA courses at competitive rates. We are allowing flexible study schedules. Our experienced mentors offer specialized support to maximize your learning experience.

Do we genuinely have unlimited viewing of lectures?

Yes, as part of our commitment to student success, we have provided unlimited access to our sessions. This means that students can revisit the lectures as many times as needed to reinforce their understanding and grasp complex concepts effectively.

How we support?

What kind of support you can expect?

Complete support is provided by our team for aspects listed below,

  • ACCA registration
  • exemption
  • Subject selection
  • Doubt solving
  • Exam booking

Is mentor support available for questions and doubts?

Depending on the package you opted, queries will be solved through chat or over a zoom call.

Study material and lecture

Is there proprietary study material available from Career Compass, and how does it contribute to exam success?

Yes, Career Compass provides comprehensive study materials meticulously crafted to enhance exam preparation.

With clear explanations, illustrative examples, and practice questions, offering students the most relevant and up-to-date content, students can effectively grasp the concepts needed to excel in their exams.

Access the Lectures / sessions

Our lectures and sessions are conveniently accessible on our website and mobile app, compatible with both laptops and mobile devices (Android or IOS) for seamless viewing.

Study materials formats

We provide comprehensive study materials in PDF format, offering the flexibility to print and access them offline.

System Requirement

What are the system prerequisites for accessing the sessions?

You don't need to worry about system requirements for accessing the lectures. Whether you're using an Android mobile phone or a laptop.

There are no restrictions for students changing their mobile or laptop devices. All they need to do is sign in with the mobile number they used for registration on our portal.
