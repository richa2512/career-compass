Refund Policy

Last updated: February 11, 2025

Thank you for choosing Career Compass for your ACCA coaching needs. We are committed to providing you with high-quality education and services. Please read our refund policy carefully.

Refund Eligibility:

1. Refunds are Only Issued for Non-Performance:

We understand that circumstances may change, and sometimes unforeseen situations arise. Therefore, a refund will only be considered if Career Compass, fails to provide the agreed-upon service as outlined during the admission process.

2. Non-Fulfillment of Commitment:

A refund will be processed if Career Compass is unable to fulfil its commitment to provide the specified coaching services, including course materials, live sessions, and other promised resources.

Non-Refundable Situations:

1. No Refunds After Admission and Payment:

Once admission is confirmed, and payment is made, no refunds will be issued under any circumstances except as outlined in the "Refund Eligibility" section above.

2. Student's Discontinuation:

Refunds will not be granted if a student voluntarily discontinues the course or withdraws for any reason.

3. Change of Circumstances:

We understand that personal circumstances may change, but refunds will not be provided due to changes in personal situations, job commitments, relocation, or any other reasons beyond our control.

Refund Request Process:

1. Submit a Written Request:

To initiate a refund request, the student must submit a written request clearly outlining the reasons for the refund, along with any supporting documentation.

2. Review and Approval:

All refund requests will be thoroughly reviewed by Career Compass. Refunds will only be approved if the circumstances meet the criteria outlined in the "Refund Eligibility" section.

3. Refund Processing:

If a refund is approved, the processing time may vary. Refunds will be issued using the same payment method used for the original transaction.

Refunds will be processed within 10-12 working days from the date of request approval.
